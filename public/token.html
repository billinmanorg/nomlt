What is a token?

A token is a digital unit recorded on a blockchain. Software can use it to track a balance and apply rules for access, payments or participation.

Think of a credit you use in an app or a pass that unlocks a service. A token can serve a similar purpose, but its rules and transactions can be recorded on a blockchain. What it actually does depends on the system built around it.

A balance

A digital wallet can hold tokens. A connected app can check the balance when you choose to use a token-based feature.

A set of rules

A smart contract is software on a blockchain. It can define how tokens move, while the app defines which services they can access.

A useful action

An app might accept tokens for a service or check that you hold enough to access a membership. Those uses must be built and enabled.

A token does not automatically represent company ownership, guarantee a reward or give access to someone’s data. Its purpose comes from the published rules and working applications behind it.

The technology behind the Angel ecosystem.

Angel Twin describes its technology as built on AngelAi’s intelligence and Transactional Language Model (TLM), together with Twin Protocol’s decentralized AI technology. NOLMT’s direction is to explore how those capabilities could support AI apps, agent services and customer-created tokens.

Angel Wallet

Angel Twin describes a wallet for managing documents, media and conversation history with encrypted, distributed storage. The NOLMT opportunity is to connect relevant information to an AI experience with its owner’s permission.

Any token or payment features would need a confirmed integration and their own terms. Storing information does not itself authorize spending, sharing or AI training.

Transactional Language Model

A TLM helps connect what someone says with structured information and software actions. In plain English: understand the request, identify the steps and work through defined systems.

For NOLMT, the proposed use is an agent workflow with clear permissions and costs, including approval before a payment or other consequential action.

What could NOLMT power?

The goal is to make tokens useful inside the AI experiences people already want to use. These capabilities are in development.

AI app and agent usage

Use tokens to pay for selected AI features, agent tasks or services inside participating apps, subject to the service’s published pricing and terms.

Access and membership

Connect token-based access to selected tools, content or membership experiences. Each program would define its own eligibility and benefits.

Agent-to-agent payments

Enable an agent to request a service from another agent and arrange payment within a budget and permission set by the user. This is a future capability.

For example, an app could quote a token price for an AI task. You review the cost, approve the action and receive a record of the completed service. Availability, fees and transaction rules would be shown before use.
