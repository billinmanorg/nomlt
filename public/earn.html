The rules

How this stays honest.

Anything with value attracts people who want it for free. These are the controls that make farming NOLMT more expensive than it is worth.

Verified accounts only

Rewards are tied to a real, verified account. Nothing meaningful becomes claimable anonymously.

Validated on the server

A click in a browser is never proof that anything happened. The server decides, every time.

Caps and cooldowns

Per action, per day, per week, per lifetime — and per device and wallet where it matters.

Duplicate detection

Repeat emails, devices, wallets, IP patterns and referral clusters are flagged and reviewed.

Risk scoring, not bans

Weighted signals decide between normal, extra verification and a review hold. One weak signal never blocks you outright.

Reversible before claim

Fraudulent pending rewards can be invalidated before they are claimed, with an audit trail.

What we will never reward.

Handing over sensitive personal information. No social security numbers, financial credentials, medical data, passwords or government IDs. Rewards recognise participation, learning and contribution — nothing else.

Claiming

Off-chain until you say otherwise.

Rewards accumulate as ledger entries, not blockchain transactions. That keeps it free, fast and reversible if something is wrong. When you want the tokens in your own wallet, you connect one and claim.

A claim needs a verified account, a connected wallet, a minimum balance and a clean risk check. Claims are authorised server-side — the treasury never takes instructions from a browser.
