Why an account

The boring answer is the honest one.

One person, one balance

Without accounts, one script becomes ten thousand participants overnight and the rewards mean nothing.

A reason for every entry

Rewards are tied to actions you took, so your history explains itself rather than being a mystery number.

Reversible before claim

If something is wrong, a pending reward can be corrected. After a blockchain transfer, it cannot.

Your wallet stays optional

Connect one when you are ready to claim — not to sign up, and not to participate.

What we do not ask for. No government ID, no financial credentials, no medical data, no social security number. Rewards recognise participation, not personal information.
