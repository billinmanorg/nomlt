Community App · $6,000

Member training platform

A professional association with 1,200 members, three certification tracks and a monthly event calendar that used to live in email.

Members sign in once. Course progress, certificates, event bookings and the member directory sit behind that single login. An assistant answers questions from the association's own handbook rather than the open internet, and says so when it does not know.

Completion carries utility: finishing a track credits the member's balance, which unlocks the next tier of material and priority event seats.

Service business front door
