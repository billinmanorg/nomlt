Tokenization

Turn participation into utility.

A loyalty scheme rewards you for spending. A tokenized community rewards you for taking part — learning, showing up, contributing, referring — and then gives that reward somewhere to go.

It is closer to airline points than to a currency market, except the balance is yours, the rules are published, and the same token can work across everything you build.
