San Diego Home Buyers

A full home-buying site that walks a visitor from browsing listings to holding a pre-approval letter, without ever leaving the page.

For: real estate agents and mortgage teams who want buyers ready before the first call

  • Four guided steps: search listings, request a showing, pre-qualify, get pre-approved
  • Live affordability calculator with conventional, FHA, and VA options
  • Text alerts for new listings and price drops, plus English and Spanish
  • Every step captures the lead and routes it to the agent

  • Market assumptions the agent sets once and sees applied everywhere
  • Personal invite links, so each recruit gets their own starting point
