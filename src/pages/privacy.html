This Privacy Policy explains how Decentralized Technologies, Inc., operating as dectec ("we," "us," or "our"), handles personal information in connection with the Twin Portal and Twin services and technology it provides. Personal information means information that identifies, relates to, or can reasonably be linked to a person or household, as applicable law defines it.

The Services are offered to eligible U.S. adults age 18 and older and U.S. organizations. U.S. customer eligibility does not mean all processing necessarily occurs in the United States. Our Terms of Service address service use; this Policy is a notice of data practices, not a waiver of privacy rights or a substitute for consent required for a particular activity.

1. Scope and our role

This Policy covers personal information processed through dectec-provided Twin Portal accounts, Twin Vault, file storage and search, Ask AI, chat, AI twins and supported voice/avatar features, content tools, authorized agents, integrations, APIs, SDKs, subscriptions, points, payment administration, support, and related commercial tools. It applies to personal information about individual customers, organizational users, partner contacts, prospects, and people whose information is lawfully supplied to an enabled service.

It does not automatically govern an independent company's website, app, model, wallet, or payment service simply because it integrates with us or uses Twin branding. That provider's role, agreement, and privacy practices also matter. Nor does it cover employment records of dectec's own personnel where a separate personnel notice applies.

We determine how to process account, billing, support, security, and business-contact information for our service operations. Where we process an organization's hosted content solely on its documented instructions, our role may instead be that of a service provider or processor under the applicable contract and law. The organization remains responsible for its own notices, instructions, and decisions. We assist with requests as required; we do not require every user to resolve all privacy concerns with a partner before contacting us.

2. Information we collect and its sources

We collect information from you, your authorized users, a properly authorized organization or provisioning partner, the devices and browsers used to access the Services, connected services you authorize, and service or transaction providers supporting your use. We do not treat account provisioning as consent to every optional disclosure or marketing activity.

2.1 Account and business details. Name, email, ordinary contact details, organization, role, account identifiers, account preferences, eligibility statements, invitations, administrative assignments, and authentication or access records. Registration and recovery processes can involve information that law treats as sensitive, even though general sensitive-content uploads are prohibited.

2.2 Customer Content. Uploaded or connected documents, files, ordinary contact records, prompts, conversations, instructions, knowledge bases, authorized brand materials, and supported media. Processing may produce extracted text, transcripts, summaries, indexes, embeddings, source references, and AI outputs. Content may concern you or others; submit it only with appropriate authority and within the restrictions in Section 3.

2.3 AI and agent activity. Requests and answers; chosen models and tools; connected-account identifiers and granted permissions; schedules and task status; actions taken on files or through integrations; interaction or consent records; and usage and error information. Where a configured feature performs matching, recommendations, or personalization, outputs may include inferred preferences or assessments based on the permitted inputs. Those assessments are not necessarily accurate.

2.4 Credentials supplied through approved connections. A designated connection feature may receive a scoped API key or access token that you authorize us to use. That is different from uploading passwords, private keys, or seed phrases as general content. Credential handling and connected-provider processing are described in Section 5.

2.5 Commercial and payment records. Plans, purchases, invoices, billing contact details, subscription events, points balances and usage, transaction identifiers, payment status, and dispute or refund records. Cryptocurrency payments may involve public wallet addresses, asset and network information, transaction hashes, amounts, and confirmations. Exact payment fields received by dectec versus an independent processor depend on the implemented payment flow; do not assume we receive complete card data or control a customer's wallet.

2.6 Portal activity and analytics. Page views, document downloads, calculator entries, demo interactions, conversations with portal assistants, IP addresses, browser and device details, timestamps, navigation, interaction events, and session recordings or replays. These records can be associated with a signed-in account and organization. Section 7 explains tracking and required controls.

2.7 Communications. Support messages, privacy requests, feedback voluntarily submitted to us, sales and demo inquiries, preferences for follow-up, and correspondence needed to resolve a request. Audio/video recording of an actual call or meeting is separate from web session replay and requires appropriate notice and consent where applicable.

2.8 Additional sources and future data products. We may receive transaction status from a payment service or permitted content from a service you connect. A future audience-data enrichment or licensing program is not authorized by a blanket reference to partners. Before adding such a program, we must establish the necessary data rights and provide applicable disclosures and consent choices. This Policy does not represent that any contemplated audience-data provider or technology integration is already operating.

3. Sensitive information, media, and information about minors

Do not place government identification numbers or scans, financial access credentials, full payment-card data or security codes, medical records, highly sensitive health data, genetic information, biometric identifiers/templates, precise location histories, children's personal information, intimate personal records, private wallet keys, or seed phrases into general Vault content, prompts, chat, or support messages.

Ordinary registration details and information submitted in an expressly designated payment or scoped-credential connection flow are limited exceptions for those purposes. These flows are not permission to upload unrelated sensitive material.

Voice recordings, images, and identifiable avatars must be supported by the rights and authorizations required for the relevant feature. A general content license does not authorize undisclosed biometric derivation, voiceprint identification, or face-template processing. Where such processing is involved, separate feature disclosures, consent where required, and retention rules must be in place before collection.

For authorized voice and avatar features, submitted recordings, images, scripts, and related service data may be processed by dectec and the providers supporting the selected feature to produce the requested voice, avatar, or Twin experience. Use is limited to that authorized purpose, service support, security, and legal obligations. A separate identification, biometric-template, or recognition use is not permitted under the general content license; it requires the legally necessary feature-specific disclosures and authorization before collection or processing. You may request that an enabled feature be stopped and associated data deleted through the email address in Appendix A. Section 9 applies to retained copies, subject to any shorter biometric or other statutory period.

The Services are not directed to people under 18, and minors may not create or use accounts under these Terms. Do not provision an account for a child or upload a child's personal information. If we learn that information has been collected contrary to these restrictions, we will take appropriate steps to restrict processing and delete information as required by law. Report a concern through Section 15 without unnecessarily repeating the sensitive material. Prohibiting an upload does not remove legal protections from information we actually receive.

4. Why we use information

We use personal information for the following purposes, as relevant to the requested Service and permitted by law:

Service delivery. Creating and administering accounts; storing, organizing, finding, and retrieving authorized content; producing requested AI outputs; providing supported Twin experiences; and carrying out authorized instructions and integrations.

Account and partner administration. Associating a user with an organization, maintaining authorized permissions, supporting provisioned accounts, implementing service configurations, and communicating about access or the applicable program.

Transactions and usage. Measuring service consumption, maintaining points and usage records, billing authorized charges, recording crypto payment status, responding to billing disputes, and keeping necessary financial records.

Support and business follow-up. Answering requests, providing demos, understanding a prospect's stated needs and use of portal materials, and following up about relevant services, subject to applicable communication rules and choices. Internal sales staff may use account-linked portal activity for this purpose. Partner access is limited by the actual authorized configuration and Section 6.

Reliability and protection. Troubleshooting, diagnosing failures, monitoring performance, understanding use of features, detecting abuse and fraud, protecting systems, maintaining appropriate records, and enforcing lawful service restrictions.

Legal and rights administration. Handling privacy and copyright requests, satisfying legal obligations, responding to valid legal process, and establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

Operational analytics and debugging are distinct from training a model for unrelated users, creating a commercial dataset, or advertising uses. Those practices require the specific disclosure in Section 5 or 8; this purpose list does not supply blanket permission for them.

5. AI providers, API keys, and model-training uses

5.1 Requested AI processing. When you ask a question, generate a Twin output, or authorize an agent, the service may retrieve permitted information and transmit the request, relevant context, tool results, and necessary metadata to the selected model or service provider. The output and related activity can be stored in your account. Using a Vault does not mean data never leaves it or that no provider sees the context used for a request.

5.2 User-supplied accounts. With your own LLM key, model calls use the connected provider account according to the actual configuration. That provider's account settings and terms can affect retention, usage, billing, and processing. dectec processes the credential and relevant data only within the authorized service purpose, subject to applicable duties. Use the designated connection flow and provider-side safeguards; never disclose raw credentials in general content.

Credential and provider handling. A supplied key or access token may be used and retained only for the authorized connection and necessary security or troubleshooting. Access must be limited to personnel and service components with a legitimate operational need. Credentials must not be repurposed for unrelated accounts, content, or charges. After disconnection or account closure, we stop new use and remove the usable credential from active connection systems without unnecessary delay; restricted backup or security copies follow Section 9. You should also revoke or rotate the credential at its issuing provider. Authorized model providers may receive prompts, relevant context, tool results, and metadata needed for the request. Hosting, storage, content-processing, and support providers may process the data needed for their functions. Provider and connection-handling information applicable to your Service may be requested through Appendix A before authorizing a connection. No particular encryption, zero-access architecture, or provider retention period is promised by this paragraph.

5.3 Shared-model training and secondary exploitation. Processing content to answer your request is different from training or improving a general model for other customers, licensing a dataset, or exploiting your content for unrelated advertising. The content license in the Terms is not blanket authorization for those secondary purposes.

Under this Policy, dectec will not use Customer Content, private prompts, outputs, recordings, or likeness materials to train a general-purpose or shared model for unrelated users without a separate, specific, voluntary opt-in. Processing and adaptation needed to provide your expressly requested personal Twin or private service is distinct from that secondary training use. Operational debugging and service analytics remain limited to the purposes described here. Contracted providers processing on our behalf must be restricted to their authorized purposes. A model or other independent service you select may apply its own account terms and settings; review them before connecting it. You may withdraw a separate optional training consent prospectively through the email address in Appendix A. Withdrawal does not promise removal of information from an independently operated model beyond rights and remedies actually available under law and the applicable provider arrangement.

5.4 Agent control. Tools may read or modify content, send communications, operate connected applications, delegate work, or perform supported transactions only within the relevant authorization. Review the data scope and action permissions before enabling a task. You can withdraw authorization through supported controls and the connected provider. Revocation does not erase information already legitimately delivered or reverse an irrevocable action, but it does not authorize new unrelated processing.

5.5 Automated assessments. Recommendations and match explanations may be derived from permitted information. They are not a guarantee of a person's attributes or suitability. Where a use constitutes regulated profiling or automated decisionmaking, applicable notice, access, objection, or opt-out requirements must be met. This Policy does not by itself authorize an unreviewed significant decision about employment, credit, housing, or another regulated opportunity.

7. Cookies, session replay, and portal tracking

We track portal use including pages viewed, downloads, calculator inputs, demo activity, conversations, IP/device information, and session interactions. This can help us operate the portal, troubleshoot, understand service interest, and follow up with prospects. Session replay can reconstruct interaction sequences; it is distinct from a live human observing a call. Some tracking may be provided by third-party tools that receive the related data.

Recording limits and retention. Portal recording may capture navigation, page interactions, calculator entries, demo activity, and assistant interactions for the purposes stated above. Session replay is permitted only on screens and fields configured to exclude credential-entry fields, payment security data, and private Vault file contents. If those exclusions cannot be maintained for an area, replay must remain disabled for that area. This does not prevent separately authorized processing of content to deliver a requested AI or storage feature. Recordings must be deleted no later than 90 days after capture unless a specific legal, security, or documented dispute-preservation need requires restricted retention. Other identifiable interaction and technical logs follow Section 9. We do not promise that every prohibited sensitive upload can be detected. Tracking-provider and recording-scope information may be requested through Appendix A.

Where consent is required before recording or nonessential tracking, we will present the required notice and obtain consent before that activity begins. A general acceptance of the Terms or visit to the portal does not replace that consent. Essential security and service records are distinguished from optional recording and marketing uses.

To request information about cookies, recording, or tracking, or to withdraw optional consent, use the email address in Appendix A. No special subject line is required. We will apply applicable choices prospectively without unnecessary delay and within required legal periods. Browser controls may limit cookies but do not necessarily stop server-side records. Essential functions may depend on required technologies. An email request supplements, rather than displaces, any in-service or automated control required by applicable law.

Recognized opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control where applicable, will be processed as required for covered sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. Traditional browser "Do Not Track" signals are different; this Policy does not promise a separate technical response to every such setting. You may communicate a choice using the email address in Appendix A. Neither an opt-out signal nor an email opt-out requires discontinuing essential security records or closes your account.

9. Retention, closure, and deletion

We retain information only for the purposes described in this Policy, as necessary for the account or requested service and applicable legal obligations. The criteria differ by category:

Customer Content and derivatives. Stored while needed to provide the active account and requested features, subject to deletion instructions and legal rights. When the account closes, controllable content, extracted text, transcripts, indexes, embeddings, and associated copies are deleted within six months, except narrowly justified retention below.

Account and administrative data. Kept while necessary to administer the relationship. Personal information attributable solely to a closed account is ordinarily deleted within the same six-month ceiling unless a specific exception applies.

Credentials. Used only for the authorized connection. On disconnection or closure we stop new use and remove usable credentials from active connection systems without unnecessary delay. Restricted residual copies may remain only for necessary backup, security, or legal purposes under the applicable deletion schedule, ordinarily no longer than six months after closure unless a lawful exception applies. You should also revoke access at the provider. No retention period authorizes continued use of a disconnected key.

Portal analytics, recordings, and technical logs. Session recordings follow the 90-day maximum in Section 7. Other identifiable analytics and routine technical logs must be deleted or deidentified within 180 days of collection unless a shorter applicable period or a specific legal, security, or dispute-preservation need applies. Account-level billing, consent, support, and transaction records are governed separately below. Data attributable solely to a closed account remains subject to the six-month closure ceiling where sooner, absent a justified exception. Deidentified information may be retained only with measures and commitments required to keep it deidentified.

Billing, consent, request, and dispute records. Limited records may be retained beyond account closure as required or reasonably necessary for taxes, accounting, fraud prevention, proof of authorization, privacy- request handling, legal claims, or a valid preservation obligation. They must remain restricted to the justified purpose and be deleted when no longer necessary.

Backups and supplier copies. The deletion process must include controllable backups and instructions to contracted suppliers, within the applicable period and subject to legitimate legal exceptions. Restoring a backup must not defeat a valid deletion. Public blockchain records and information independently retained by authorized external recipients may not be erasable by us; that does not excuse deletion of copies we control or required instructions to service providers.

Six months is an ordinary account-closure ceiling, not a minimum retention period. A statutory deletion or other privacy request may require action sooner. Closing an organizational account does not authorize destruction of another customer's independently owned account information.
