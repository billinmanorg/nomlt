Design questions

What we work out together.

Before anything is issued, these five answers have to exist in writing.

01

Who is a member?

What verification is proportionate — email, phone, an existing membership number, an invitation.

02

What is worth rewarding?

The three to five actions that genuinely grow the community, and what each is worth.

03

What can they unlock?

The utility catalogue on day one. Not a roadmap — real things, available at launch.

04

What is the budget?

Campaign allocation, daily distribution limits and per-member caps, set before anyone earns anything.

05

Who can pause it?

Named people with the authority to stop rewards or claims immediately, and the audit trail behind them.

And

What happens if it fails?

How balances are honoured, how members are told, and how the thing is wound down without damaging your reputation.
